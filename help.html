Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I find schemes I'm eligible for?
You can use our 'Discover Schemes' tool on the homepage or dashboard. By setting up your profile with your age, state, and occupation, the portal automatically recommends schemes tailored for you.
2. How do I apply for Ayushman Bharat?
You can check your eligibility on the PMJAY portal (pmjay.gov.in) using your mobile number or ration card. If eligible, you can get your e-card generated at the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or empanelled hospital.
3. What is PM-KISAN and who is eligible?
PM-KISAN is a central sector scheme that provides ₹6,000 per year (in 3 installments) to all landholding farmer families. You can apply directly on the pmkisan.gov.in portal.
4. How do I get a birth certificate?
Birth registration is handled by local authorities (Municipal Corporation/Gram Panchayat). You can apply through your respective State e-District portal or crsorgi.gov.in.
5. How do I register my business as MSME?
You can register for Udyam Registration completely free of cost on the official udyamregistration.gov.in portal using only your Aadhaar and PAN card.
6. What documents do I need for a passport?
Generally, you need Proof of Address (like Aadhaar, Voter ID) and Proof of Date of Birth (like Birth Certificate, 10th Marks card). Visit passportindia.gov.in for the exact list.
